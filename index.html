من نحن · About the Group

Forty-five years of building

Al-Baz Group is a Baghdad-based Iraqi industrial family. Since 1980 we have built companies across food, tobacco, hookah supplies, real-estate investment and hospitality — employing hundreds of Iraqis.

Our vision

The Al-Saloumi Industrial Family is a symbol of quality and success, renowned for innovation and skill in managing its projects. Its long history in trade and industry reflects a dedication to success, continuity and hard work.

We believe in building in Iraq, with Iraqi hands. Now in its second generation, the group is managed with a long view — investing in people and ventures for the next forty years.

Milestones
1980

Al-Baz founded in Baghdad; the first production line starts and the Baz brand is born.

1990s

Food production expands with Al-Baz Ready-to-Eat Foods Ltd.

2000s

Entry into premium tobacco through Al-Baz Molasses Tobacco Production.

2018

Modern Visions launches in hookahs & supplies trading, alongside Tayeb Al-Khair for real estate.

Today

Five companies, with the newest addition — Beethoven Restaurant & Café in the heart of Baghdad.

What we stand for

Quality first

Every product and service carries the family name. Nothing leaves us that we wouldn't offer at our own table.

Iraqi enterprise

We build in Iraq, with Iraqi hands. Local employment and local suppliers are at the core of every decision.

Continuity

Through decades of change, Al-Baz never stopped. Those who build with us build on ground that holds.

Family stewardship

Now in its second generation, the group is managed with a long view — investing in people and ventures.

Al-Baz Ltd. for Ready-to-Eat Foods

شركة باز لإنتاج الأطعمة الجاهزة المحدودة

The company that started it all. Founded in 1980 and still a leader in high-quality ready-to-eat foods — specializing in chips and savory snacks, alongside a range of natural fruit juices. Al-Baz emphasizes fresh, locally-sourced ingredients, is recognized among Iraq's leading food companies, and exports its products to several Arab and foreign countries.

  • A wide range: chips, popcorn, Tarbosh, Max, Mix and juices
  • Modern production lines with daily quality checks
  • Fresh, locally-sourced, sustainable ingredients
  • Distribution across Iraq and export abroad
